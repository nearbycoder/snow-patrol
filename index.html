Tactile Snow Displacement & Rolling Wave

Your hydraulic plow doesn't just erase white tiles — it scoops powder into 15 live load bins, builds a churning rolling wave in front of the steel moldboard, casts angled windrow berms with Q/E, leaves corduroy grooves behind the rear tiller, and lets you lift the blade with Space.

1px Outlines, Bayer Dithering & Snow Caps

Renders to an adjustable low-res WebGL framebuffer (1x HD to 4x Chunky) with nearest-neighbor scaling, depth-edge silhouette outlines, emissive bloom for amber beacons & chalet windows, Bayer 4×4 dithered skies, and dynamic upward-facing normZ snow caps.

Built as a single-file Three.js WebGL game exploring procedural volumetric sprite-stacking, deformable snow physics, Web Audio synthesis, and zero-shot neural voice cloning.
