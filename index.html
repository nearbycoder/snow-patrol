Built Like a Craft Indie Gem

Volumetric Sprite-Stacking Meets Real-Time Snow Physics

Every pixel, slice, snow bank, and synth note is generated procedurally in pure Three.js & Web Audio — zero external 3D model files or game engine bloat.

danielwh2/sprite-stacking Architecture

True 3D Volumetric Slice-Stacking

Every asset — the articulated 4-part Snowcat, Chalets, Overhead Gondolas, Barnaby the Husky, Pines, Boulders, and the Rescue Helicopter — is built as ground-to-roof 2D pixel slice strips with computed 3D occupancy normals, upper-neighbor ambient occlusion, emissive masks, and upward-facing normZ snow caps. Press V in-game to inspect live slice strips or X to explode every stack in 3D.

128×128 Heightfield Simulation

Tactile Snow Displacement & Rolling Wave

Snow doesn't just erase on contact — your hydraulic blade scoops powder into 15 live load bins, builds a churning rolling snow wave right in front of the moldboard, casts angled windrow berms sideways with Q/E, leaves corduroy tracks behind your rear tiller, and lets you lift the blade (Space) to reposition cleanly.

Crisp Pixel-Art Pipeline

Low-Res Render + 1px Outlines

Renders to an adjustable low-res WebGL target (1x HD to 4x Chunky) with nearest-neighbor blitting, depth-edge 1px silhouette ink outlines, emissive pixel bloom, Bayer 4×4 dithered sunset & aurora skies, and live lantern snow pools.

Web Audio API Synthesizer

Adaptive Synth + Formant Choir

Three time-of-day soundtracks (Alpenglow Chill, Alpine Groove, Midnight Aurora) with real vowel-formant vocal choir pads, dynamic percussion that builds as you plow, mountain wind ambience, diesel engine purr, and brass air horn.

Full Settings Menu (O)

Deep Audio & Visual Tuning

Tune Master, Music, Radio & SFX sliders, switch Radio EQ (Broadcast / Walkie / Studio), adjust pixel scale, shadow quality, outline & bloom intensity, snow-cap depth, and camera sensitivity — all saved automatically.
